Medical & Editorial Review Policy

Pet health is a "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) topic. We treat accuracy as the product.

1. Our sourcing standards

We ground guidance in authoritative, primary sources wherever possible, including:

• AAHA — American Animal Hospital Association guidelines
• VCA Animal Hospitals — clinical pet care library
• AKC — American Kennel Club breed & care resources
• AVMA — American Veterinary Medical Association
• FDA / CVM — U.S. Food & Drug Administration Center for Veterinary Medicine (recalls, food safety)

2. Our review model

Plan A — Named licensed veterinarian (preferred)

When a licensed DVM is engaged for review, the article carries a reviewedBy Person schema with the reviewer's name and credentials. This is the strongest trust signal for both readers and search/AI systems.

Plan B — Editorial & Medical Review Team (current default)

Until a named reviewer is engaged, articles are reviewed by our editorial team against the sourcing standards above. We do not present anonymous health advice as if it came from a specific named veterinarian. The article schema reflects reviewedBy: Organization.

3. What a guide is — and is not

Not a diagnosis. Our guides provide general education only. They do not replace a veterinarian's examination, diagnosis, or treatment plan. If your pet shows severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms, contact a licensed veterinarian or an emergency animal hospital immediately.

4. Corrections

We correct errors promptly. Flag a concern at pets@goods-infinite.com and we will review and update the article as needed.

Who reviews GoodsInfinite Pet's content for medical accuracy?

Articles are researched by our editorial team and reviewed for medical accuracy before publication. When a licensed veterinarian (DVM) is engaged, the article carries a named reviewer; otherwise it is reviewed by our Editorial & Medical Review Team and labeled accordingly.

What sources does GoodsInfinite Pet rely on?

We ground guidance in authoritative sources such as AAHA, VCA Animal Hospitals, the American Kennel Club (AKC), the AVMA, and the FDA Center for Veterinary Medicine.

Can a GoodsInfinite Pet guide replace a veterinarian?

No. Our guides provide general education only and are not a diagnosis or treatment plan. For severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms, contact a licensed veterinarian or emergency animal hospital immediately.
