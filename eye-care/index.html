👁️ Tear Stains & Eye Care for Dogs and Cats

Gentle cleansers, wipes and drops for the under-eye zone — where crust, moisture and staining meet.

Tear staining — the rust-colored marks under the eyes — is one of the most searched pet grooming problems, especially for light-coated breeds like Maltese, Shih Tzus and Persians. It's usually cosmetic, but the moisture involved deserves consistent care.

What causes tear staining?

Stains form when tears overflow onto the fur (epiphora) and pigments called porphyrins in the tears oxidize in sunlight, turning reddish-brown. Contributing factors include shallow tear ducts, eyelash issues, flat-faced anatomy, allergies, and irritation from dust or dander.

Sources: VCA Animal Hospitals — tear duct and eye disorders; Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine.

The 4-step daily routine

  1. Flush — an eye wash (sterile-style rinse) clears dust and discharge without touching the eye directly.
  2. Dissolve — a stain-remover liquid applied to the fur (never in the eye) lifts existing buildup.
  3. Wipe — hypoallergenic wipes dry the area; moisture is what breeds yeast and deepens staining.
  4. Maintain — keep the zone dry with grooming wipes 2–3×/week once stains fade.
Consistency beats strength: a gentle daily wipe-down outperforms aggressive scrubbing, which irritates skin and can worsen tearing.

When staining means 'call the vet'

See a veterinarian if you notice squinting, redness of the eye itself, pawing at the face, sudden heavy discharge, or one-eye-only tearing — those point to medical causes (blocked ducts, ingrown lashes, ulcers) that no cosmetic product should mask.

FAQs — eye care & tear stains

Are tear stains harmful to my pet?

Usually cosmetic, but the constant moisture under the eyes can breed yeast and bacteria, causing odor and skin irritation. Regular cleaning keeps the area healthy.

Can I use the stain remover in my pet's eye?

No. Stain removers and wipes are for the fur around the eye. Use the dedicated eye wash or drops for anything near or in the eye itself.

How long until stains fade?

Fur must grow out and shed the stained hair, so expect 3–8 weeks of daily care. Keeping the area dry speeds it up dramatically.

Do white-faced breeds always have this problem?

Breeds like Maltese, Bichons and Persians are predisposed due to facial anatomy and tear drainage, so maintenance is lifelong — but daily wiping keeps it manageable.
